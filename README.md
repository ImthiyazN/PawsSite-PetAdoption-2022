# Paws Site - Pet Adoption & Local Services Platform

A community-focused web platform prototype designed to streamline pet adoption, facilitate charitable donations, connect pet owners with local pet care vendors, and gather community feedback.

---

## Key Features

* **Pet Adoption Directory:** Displays available pets looking for homes with essential adoption details.
* **Services & Store Locator:** Helps pet owners locate nearby pet stores and professional grooming services.
* **Donations & Feedback System:** Integrated user forms for supporting local animal welfare and collecting site feedback.
* **About & Information Hub:** Overview of the platform's community mission and pet care initiatives.

---

## Tech Stack

* **Structure:** HTML5
* **Styling & Layout:** CSS3
* **Assets & Visuals:** Custom image assets and media integration
